# Outreach kit

Short messages for the people Still Recalled is for. Each is written to send as is: fill in the brackets and send it
from your own email or account. Nothing here has been sent. Contacts and the reasons each one fits are in
[Who to contact](#who-to-contact) below; every address there was found on the organization's own site.

What to ask for is the same each time, and small: **try it on a few real items and tell us what happened**. A reply,
a pilot at one sale, or a few families using the card is real evidence that it helps, which is worth more to this
project than any new feature. Replies can go to the public feedback form,
https://github.com/usv240/still-recalled/issues/new?template=feedback.yml, or straight to you.

The facts used below, with sources: the census in `docs/accuracy.md` (sections 29 and 31), the law in
`docs/research/sources.md` (sections 1 and 2).

> Would you be willing to look at the card and tell me whether it fits what you teach? If it does, I would be glad

## 4. A parent community post

Post only where the rules allow it (see the notes under each community below), from your own account, and answer
questions in the thread yourself. Keep it as useful without the link as with it.

**Title:** I built a free check for recalled baby gear on Marketplace and eBay. Would you try it on a real listing?

> Recalls reach the first owner, not the person who buys it used. I made a free tool for that: add a screenshot of
> a listing, or install the Chrome extension, and it checks it against every CPSC recall notice since 1973. It also
> flags the sleep products that are banned or that CPSC warns against (inclined sleepers, padded bumpers, loungers),
> even when the title doesn't name them.
>
> https://still-recalled.web.app (no account, nothing to install). There's also a printable card in English and
> Spanish.
>
> It never says something is safe: no match only means no recall was found. I'd really like to hear where it gets
> things wrong. Thanks.
