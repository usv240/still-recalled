    Shopping on an Android phone?

    Get the free app and share any listing to it, Facebook Marketplace and OfferUp included: tap Share on the listing, or share a screenshot of it. It can also watch what you bought and notify you if it's recalled later.

    1. Tap Download the Android app. If that doesn't start, use this copy on our site.
    2. Your phone warns you because the app isn't from the Play Store. That's expected: tap Download anyway.
    3. Open the download. When asked, turn on Allow from this source, then tap Install.

    Shopping on a computer?

    The Chrome extension checks every listing on eBay, Mercari and Craigslist search pages as you scroll, and puts a warning on the ones that match. Chrome's built-in model looks at each baby product on your computer; only what it flags or can't settle, about one listing in four in our tests, is double-checked in the cloud.
