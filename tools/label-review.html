An inclined sleeper is made for a baby to sleep in, lying back at a tilt of more than 10 degrees (the law's definition). It is a bed: usually a deep fabric cradle slung in a folding or rocking frame, sold as a "sleeper", "napper" or "rock and play". A bouncer, swing or rocker seat also tilts, but it is a seat for a baby who is awake, usually with a harness between the legs and often a toy bar. Tilted is not enough: answer "Inclined sleeper" only for a bed made for sleep.
