# Still Recalled: verified sources

Researched 2026-11-15. US sources only. Quotes are verbatim and under 15 words. "Undated" means the page shows no date (accessed 2026-11-15). Each fact was checked against the source text. Anything I couldn't verify is labelled.

## Corrections to the brief (read first)

1. **Name of the Act.** Its official short title is the *Safe Sleep for Babies Act of 2021* (H.R. 3182). It was signed May 16, 2022 as Public Law 117-126. Calling it "of 2022" is wrong.
2. **The crib bumper ban covers more than padded bumpers.** It also covers vinyl bumper guards and vertical crib slat covers. Only non-padded mesh crib liners are excluded.
3. **The rule numbers are different.** Infant support cushions (loungers, positioners, props) are **16 CFR 1243**. That is a final rule, effective May 5, 2025. Part 1242 is nursing pillows. The infant sleep products rule is **16 CFR 1236**, published June 23, 2021 and effective June 23, 2022.
4. **in_bed_sleeper is not automatically illegal to resell.** Parts 1236 and 1243 cover only products made after their effective dates. Inclined sleepers and crib bumpers are different: they are banned regardless of manufacture date. So a non-recalled in-bed sleeper, nest or lounger made before 2022/2025 is *not* federally banned from resale, though CPSC warns against it. It *is* barred by Facebook Marketplace and eBay policy. A recalled one (e.g., Boppy) is illegal to sell.
5. **cpsc.gov/SafeSleep does not mention drop-side cribs.** Cite CPSC's "Stopping the Online Sale of Recalled Products" page or the Resellers Guide instead (below).
6. **CDC's latest SUID count is about 3,400 (2024), not 3,700.**
7. **Crib resale has a legal nuance.** The crib-specific statute names manufacturers, distributors and people "in the business of" selling or reselling cribs. CPSC's guidance goes further: it calls pre-2011 cribs "illegal to sell" and tells consumers they "should not resell" one. Safe wording: "CPSC says cribs made before June 28, 2011, including drop-sides, are very likely illegal to sell."

### Notes for the build (my inferences from the sources above)

- **Keyword matching on names is not enough.** Platforms already do it, and CR (2019, 2026) shows listings still get through. Sellers use generic names like "baby sleeper/rocker". Matching on *category* (incline, in-bed sleeper, bumper, drop-side) as well as recall names is the gap the sources point to.
- **Word the flag as "may be", not as a verdict.** A recalled item can be sold legally once the remedy has been applied. Pre-2022 in-bed sleepers and loungers aren't federally banned unless recalled.
- **Use the strongest legal line only for inclined_sleeper, crib_bumper and recalled items.** Those are illegal to sell regardless of age. Drop-side cribs: "CPSC says very likely illegal to sell."
