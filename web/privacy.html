Still Recalled

Privacy

Updated November 16, 2026. This covers the Still Recalled Chrome extension and the web page at still-recalled.web.app.

What stays on your computer

The extension reads the listings on eBay, Mercari and Craigslist search pages you open. Every listing is first checked by word rules and, when your computer has it, by Chrome's built-in model (Gemini Nano), both on your computer. Listings these steps clear never leave it. Your searches and the rest of your browsing are not sent anywhere.

What is sent to the cloud, and why

When a listing is flagged on your computer, or your computer can't settle it (a bedding set that may hide a bumper, a possible part, or a product whose photo suggests a recalled brand its title doesn't name), its title, description, price and photo are sent, with the CPSC recall notice it might match, to Gemini through Google's Firebase AI Logic, so a larger model can confirm or clear it. In our tests this was about one listing in four. On the web page and in the Android app, which check one listing at a time, the screenshot or text you add is read on your device when it has a built-in model, otherwise by Gemini in the cloud, and every baby product you check gets the cloud's look.

You can turn the cloud check off in the extension's side panel settings. A second setting, off unless you turn it on, sends every baby product on a page for the cloud's look, as the web page does. Google processes these requests under Firebase's data processing terms.

What is not collected

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no data sold or shared.
  • No personal information: names, addresses, payment details or messages.

What is stored

The extension keeps its results for each page in your browser for 7 days, so a page you return to isn't checked again, and keeps your settings. If you choose Watch for recalls on a listing, its title, price, photo address and link are kept in your browser so it can be checked against new recalls; you can stop watching it in the side panel. Nothing is stored on a server. Removing the extension removes them.

New recalls

Twice a day the extension asks the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's public recall API for recalls published since its list was built, and reads CPSC's public file of safety warnings. The requests contain no information about you. Gemini, through Firebase, reads each new recall notice (a public government document) to write a short summary used for matching. Your watch list is checked on your computer; a watched listing is sent to the cloud only if your computer flags it against a new recall, as described above.

Verification

To protect the project's model quota, Google's reCAPTCHA Enterprise and Firebase App Check confirm that requests come from this app. reCAPTCHA runs on still-recalled.web.app, including the small page the extension uses for this; Google's privacy policy applies to it.

Questions
