Still Recalled Chrome extension

Check a secondhand listing before you buy

Add a screenshot of the listing. Still Recalled compares it with U.S. recall notices and flags baby sleep products that put infants at risk.

    Shopping on a computer?

    The Chrome extension checks every listing on eBay, Mercari and Craigslist search pages as you scroll, and puts a warning on the ones that match. Chrome's built-in model looks at each listing on your computer; only the few it flags are double-checked in the cloud.
